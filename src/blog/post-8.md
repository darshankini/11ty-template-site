Clean code is read far more than it is written. A few habits keep your codebase maintainable for years.

## Introduction

Welcome to this article on **The Art of Writing Clean Code**. In this post we explore the core
ideas, walk through practical examples, and share tips you can apply to your own
projects right away.

## Key Points

- Understand the fundamentals before reaching for tools.
- Keep things simple and iterate as you learn.
- Measure the impact of every change you make.

## Going Deeper

Once the basics feel comfortable, experiment with real projects. Nothing builds
understanding faster than shipping something and learning from the result. Take
notes, review your work, and refine your approach over time.
